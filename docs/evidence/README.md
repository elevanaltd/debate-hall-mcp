## Key Implications

**For debate-hall-mcp users:**

1. **Use dialectic, not iteration** - The explore→constrain→synthesize structure produces emergent solutions that pure iteration cannot achieve, even with accumulated context.

2. **Match models to cognitive modes:**
   ```
   PATHOS (Wind): Claude Opus 4.5 (96% score)
   ETHOS (Wall):  GPT-5.2        (97% score)
   LOGOS (Door):  Gemini 3 Pro   (97% score)
   ```

3. **Expect emergent properties** - The dialectic doesn't just refine ideas; it produces genuinely novel solutions like the "Staged Sovereignty Model" that transcend the original constraints.

## Evidence Standards

All studies follow rigorous methodology:

- **Blind assessment** - Responses anonymized before scoring
- **Multiple assessors** - Independent evaluation with inter-rater reliability
- **Controlled comparisons** - Equal cognitive budget across approaches
- **Quantified metrics** - Standardized rubrics with dimension breakdowns
- **Replication** - Key findings confirmed across multiple runs
