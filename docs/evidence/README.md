## Study Progression

M018 → M019 → M021 form a coherent evidence chain:

1. **M018**: Proves dialectic structure produces emergent solutions
2. **M019**: Identifies optimal model assignments by task performance
3. **M021**: Validates M019 by showing signatures describe HOW models work, not WHAT they excel at

## Evidence Standards

All studies follow rigorous methodology:

- **Blind assessment** - Responses anonymized before scoring
- **Multiple assessors** - Independent evaluation with inter-rater reliability
- **Controlled comparisons** - Same-model controls to isolate methodology effects
- **Quantified metrics** - Standardized rubrics with dimension breakdowns
