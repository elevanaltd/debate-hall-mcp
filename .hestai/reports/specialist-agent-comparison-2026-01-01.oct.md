## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Six parallel debates were conducted comparing the new specialist agents (ideator, validator, synthesizer) against their debate-hall native counterparts (-dh variants). All agents used their designated CLI tools:
- **Ideator comparison**: Gemini CLI
